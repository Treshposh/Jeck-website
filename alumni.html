    Stephanie’s story

    From self-doubt to a Master’s degree.

    Stephanie joined JECK in September 2012 as a JSS3 student. Before then, negative words at her previous school had left her doubting herself and believing she was not clever enough to succeed.

    At JECK she found teachers who were supportive, encouraging and genuinely invested in every student, and a lively classroom where healthy competition pushed everyone to do their best. She challenged herself before her Junior WAEC exams, and that decision changed everything. By senior secondary she believed in herself, and reading became a daily habit.

    1. 2012

      Joins JECK in JSS3

    2. UNIPORT

      Bachelor’s degree in Economics, University of Port Harcourt

    3. NYSC

      National Youth Service in Ebonyi State

    4. MSc

      Master’s degree in Monetary Economics

    5. Today

      Pursuing certification with the Chartered Institute of Personnel Management (CIPM)
