    Our mission

    To raise well-rounded students who fear God and show disciplined attitudes, built on solid academic and moral foundations, by providing a serene learning environment and quality teaching and learning.

    Our vision

    To raise a total child who is sound spiritually, morally, physically and academically.

    Our values

    Fear of God, discipline, excellence, community.

    Our vision is to raise a total child who is sound spiritually, morally, physically and academically. These four values guide every lesson, every assembly and every match.

    Fear of GodFaith and good conscience guide how we learn and how we treat people.
    DisciplinePunctuality, self-control and respect, every single day.
    ExcellenceWe aim high and help each student reach their best.
    CommunityHealthy competition and real friendship, so no one is left behind.
