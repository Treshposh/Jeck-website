    Academic life

    Strong foundations. Bright futures.

    From the first day of JSS1 to the final WAEC paper in SSS3, our graduate teachers guide every student with patience and high expectations.

    Graduate teachers

    Qualified teachers who explain clearly, check understanding and give extra help when a student needs it.

    Nigeria and beyond

    A standard curriculum that prepares students for higher learning anywhere in the world.

    Healthy competition

    Students push each other to do better, and no one is left behind.

    JSS 1 – 3

    Junior Secondary.

    Strong basics in English, Mathematics, the sciences and the arts, with study habits that last. Students sit the Basic Education Certificate Examination (Junior WAEC) at the end of JSS3.

    Subjects include English Language, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Social Studies, Civic Education, Computer Studies, Business Studies, Creative Arts, Christian Religious Studies, French and a Nigerian language.

    SSS 1 – 3

    Senior Secondary.

    Students choose a stream, with focused preparation for WAEC and NECO and guidance on university choices.

    Science

    Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Further Mathematics.

    Arts

    Literature, Government, History and Christian Religious Studies.

    Commercial

    Economics, Accounting and Commerce.

    Science laboratory

    Learning by doing.

    Science students carry out practical work in our laboratory, measuring, testing and recording results just as real scientists do. Practicals build confidence for WAEC and NECO and spark a love for science.

    Library and reading

    A reading culture.

    Our library is a favourite place for reading, research and quiet revision. Reading every day is a habit we build from JSS1.

    Examinations

    Ready for every exam.
