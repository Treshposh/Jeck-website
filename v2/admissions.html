    Admissions process

    Join the JECK family.

    Admissions are open. We would love to meet your child.

    1. Collect the form

      Pick up an admission form from the school office.

    2. Sit the entrance exam

      Your child writes a short exam in English and Mathematics.

    3. Meet with us

      Visit with a parent or guardian for a short, friendly interview.

    4. Resume school

      Complete registration and join your new class at the start of term.

    Book a school visit

    Come and see for yourself.

    Visit on any school day between 8am and 5pm. Tour the classrooms, laboratory and library, and meet our teachers.

    Book a visit on WhatsAppTake the virtual tour
    Entry points

    When to join.

    JSS1 entry

    Starting secondary school

    For pupils finishing primary school. Children sit the entrance exam in English and Mathematics before joining JSS1.

    SSS1 entry

    Starting senior secondary

    For students who have completed JSS3 and the BECE. New students choose the Science, Arts or Commercial stream.

    Students may also join other classes where space is available.

    Admissions FAQs

    Good to know.

    Is JECK a boys’ or girls’ school?

    Neither. JECK is co-educational, so boys and girls learn together from JSS1 to SSS3.

    Can my child join in the middle of the session?

    Yes, where there is space in the class.

    Which exams do students prepare for?

    Junior students sit the BECE (Junior WAEC) in JSS3. Senior students prepare for WAEC and NECO in SSS3.

    Is there a school bus?

    Yes. Call or WhatsApp 0913 070 4152 for routes and to register.

    Can I visit before applying?

    Of course. Come on any school day between 8am and 5pm, or book a visit on WhatsApp.
