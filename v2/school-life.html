    Assembly

    Every day starts together.

    Each morning the whole school gathers on the assembly ground for prayer, the national anthem, morning exercise and the day’s announcements. It sets the tone for a focused, disciplined day.

    Houses and sport

    March-past, races and house colours.

    Every student belongs to a house, and inter-house sports is one of the highlights of the year. Students practise their march-past for weeks, cheer for their teammates and wear their house colours with pride.

    Athletics

    Races and relays on the school field.

    March-past

    Each house trains its own squad and banner.

    Team spirit

    Friendly but fierce competition between the houses.

    Arts and culture

    Proud of where we come from.

    On cultural day students dress in traditional attire and perform dances from across Nigeria while parents and guests cheer. Drama, debate and talent shows give every student a stage all year round.

    Faith and character

    Growing in spirit and in manners.

    In line with our mission, students grow in the fear of God and in good character, learning honesty, respect and self-discipline.

    School hall

    Where we come together.

    The school hall hosts morning worship, career talks from visiting professionals, prize-giving and the yearly graduation ceremony.

    Excursions

    Lessons that leave the classroom.

    Educational trips show students how what they learn is used in the real world. They come back with stories, questions and new ambitions.

    Graduation

    Proud moments, every year.

    Each year our graduating class walks out in green and gold, ready for university and the world, surrounded by family, teachers and friends.

    School bus

    Safely to school, on time.

    The JECK school bus brings students to school safely and on time. To register your child, call or WhatsApp the school office on 0913 070 4152.
